The realm of climate technology is witnessing an invigorating mix of innovation and strategic ventures aimed at mitigating climate change. From the corridors of venture capital to the incubators of entrepreneurship, a new wave of climate tech initiatives is emerging, each carrying the promise of a greener, more sustainable future. However, this burgeoning optimism is met with a stark contrast in the form of entrenched fossil fuel interests, reminding us of the complex, multifaceted battle against climate change.

![Getty elenabs](https://techcrunch.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Silence_Collage.png?w=730&crop=1 "Getty elenabs")

**Silence's Angelic Approach to Climate Tech**:
Silence, stepping onto the stage with a $35 million fund, is carving out a niche in the climate tech investment landscape. Opting for an angel-style approach, Silence aims to sprinkle its capital across dozens of startups, betting on the agility and innovation of small teams to disrupt the status quo. Led by Borja Moreno de los Rios, a figure with a rich tapestry of tech experience, Silence aspires to apply the tech startup playbook to the climate arena, making strategic, albeit smaller, bets without seeking control or direct influence.

**Montauk Climate's Incubation Ingenuity**:
Philip Krim, leveraging his entrepreneurial zest post-Casper, is diving into the climate tech waters with Montauk Climate, an incubator with a distinct vision. Rather than casting a wide net, Montauk Climate intends to author and cultivate businesses from the ground up, focusing on asset-light and software-centric solutions. This approach underscores a strategic pivot from hardware-heavy solutions to enabling technologies that support and enhance the efficacy of existing climate solutions, from energy to infrastructure.

**Greenly's Carbon Accounting Crusade**:
Amidst the enthusiasm for innovative startups and strategic investments lies Greenly, a company with a mission to democratize carbon accounting. With a $52 million boost, Greenly's software aspires to make carbon emissions tracking accessible to small and mid-sized businesses, a demographic critically underserved in the sustainability discourse. By providing these tools, Greenly is not just facilitating compliance but fostering a culture of responsibility and action across the broader business ecosystem.

**The Fossil Fuel Conundrum**:
The forward march of climate tech, however, encounters a formidable counter-narrative from the oil and gas sector. Despite global calls for a transition to cleaner energy, leading oil executives remain steadfast in their belief in the longevity of fossil fuels. This divergence not only highlights the enduring challenge of transforming our energy systems but also the necessity of innovative solutions and strategic investments in accelerating the transition.

Among the various strategies and models presented, Greenly's approach shines as a beacon of immediate impact. By equipping SMBs with the tools to track and manage their carbon footprint, Greenly potentially catalyzes a grassroots-level shift towards sustainability, embedding climate consciousness across industries. While the investment and incubation efforts of Silence and Montauk Climate are indispensable for seeding innovation, the practical, scalable solution offered by Greenly represents a critical step towards a broader, inclusive climate action.
