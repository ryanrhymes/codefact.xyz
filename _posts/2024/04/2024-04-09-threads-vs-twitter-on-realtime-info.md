In today's digital age, social media platforms have ascended to the pinnacle of our daily interactions. Among these digital giants, Instagram Threads and Twitter (now X) stand tall, engaged in an intense battle for our attention and engagement. However, a significant difference separates them: their ability to deliver real-time information and trending topics. In this article, we delve deep into this topic, discussing the challenges Instagram Threads faces in competing with Twitter and exploring the impact on the user experience.

First, let us emphasize the importance of real-time information and trending topics in social media. Twitter became a revolutionary force during its inception, showcasing its potential when the world witnessed the platform's real-time delivery of information during the San Francisco earthquake in 2006. Founder Jack Dorsey shared his fascination with this experience, as people's shared sensation of the earthquake created a sense of unity among users.

Now, let's focus on Instagram Threads. This text-focused social network, Meta's response to Elon Musk's X, has encountered difficulties in keeping pace with Twitter. The primary causes of these challenges stem from Meta's design decisions on Threads.

First, the absence of hashtags poses a significant hurdle for Instagram Threads. Hashtags serve as a vital tool for users to join discussions on specific topics and for surfacing related posts to the wider community. However, Instagram Threads bypasses the conventional hashtag setup used on Twitter, instead advocating for the tagging convention of "[term] Threads," leading to inconsistency and potential confusion when major trending topics arise.

Second, the threading algorithm of Instagram Threads favors older posts, pushing real-time updates further down the feed. Conversely, Twitter prioritizes the most recent developments on a trend, fostering an active community where users can engage with up-to-the-minute content.

These challenges bear profound consequences for the user experience of Instagram Threads. Users expect social media platforms to keep them informed on breaking news and current events in real-time. When Instagram Threads fails to do so, the platform misses an opportunity to attract and retain a loyal user base, a stark contrast to Twitter's reputation as the standard-bearer for real-time information and trending topics.

Despite boasting a monthly active user base of 130 million and being the largest player in the "fediverse," a network of interconnected servers and services including Mastodon, Misskey, Pixelfed, and PeerTube, Instagram Threads faces steep competition. Although Twitter has experienced usage declines, the platform's connection to real-time information and trending topics has kept it relevant, ensuring a dedicated user base.

The competition between Instagram Threads and Twitter centers on their abilities to deliver real-time information and trending topics. While Instagram Threads presents a unique perspective with its clean user interface, it falls short when it comes to user engagement and immediacy. The lack of hashtags and the threading algorithm that prioritizes older posts are substantial barriers for Instagram Threads that hinder its potential to contend with Twitter. It remains intriguing to observe how Meta navigates these challenges and whether Threads can establish a distinctive identity within the social media sphere.
