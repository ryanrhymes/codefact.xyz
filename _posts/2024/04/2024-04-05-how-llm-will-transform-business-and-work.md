The last few years have seen an intense interest in Artificial Intelligence and large language models in science, business, and media. While some view this fascination as an unnecessary distraction, others see it as the leading edge of an epochal transformation. In this column, I will argue that the integration of conversational AI into our lives is the next generational technological shift, with far-reaching consequences beyond what we experienced with the adoption of spreadsheets.

![Image](https://res.cloudinary.com/dnlo7muok/image/upload/v1698159403/LLM_a77abf5a22.png "getty")

Spreadsheets, introduced in the late 1970s, revolutionized the business world by providing a powerful and flexible means for businesses to track and analyze data. They enabled companies to make informed decisions based on data, experiment with different scenarios, and gain a better understanding of their operations. This technological shift triggered an era of business innovation.

However, spreadsheets were limited in their application; they could only be used for specific types of tasks. In contrast, large language models like ChatGPT have a much broader scope of application. They can be used for a wide range of tasks, from generating text and code to answering questions, solving complex problems, and even creating art. This potentially affects various industries and sectors.

The capabilities of large language models are constantly improving, with OpenAI, the creator of ChatGPT, working on more advanced models, and AI giants like Microsoft and Google investing heavily in conversational AI. As these models become better, they will become even more powerful and capable, leading to even more transformative changes.

One of the most significant ways that large language models will transform business and work is by enabling humans to collaborate with machines in new and productive ways. Human-robot collaboration has long been a topic of research, and the integration of conversational AI into our lives is bringing this to fruition. Companies like Microsoft and Google are redesigning their product lines to incorporate conversational AI, while startups like Sierra are building agents for corporate customers that leverage multiple models. This will enable businesses to automate repetitive tasks, freeing up employees to focus on creative and complex problems.

Another way that large language models will transform businesses is by changing the way we approach problem-solving. Traditional methods involve breaking down a complex problem into smaller parts and solving each part individually. However, large language models can analyze a problem as a whole and generate multiple potential solutions, leading to more creative and innovative solutions. This could result in breakthroughs in sectors like medicine, engineering, and finance.

The integration of conversational AI into our lives has the potential to democratize access to knowledge and information. Large language models can answer questions, generate educational content, and provide recommendations based on a user's interests and preferences. This could lead to a more informed and educated population, resulting in better decision-making and a more productive economy.

The integration of conversational AI into our lives is the next generational technological shift, with far-reaching consequences for business and work. The adoption of spreadsheets revolutionized the business world, but the potential impact of large language models is even more significant. They have the potential to democratize access to knowledge and information, enable human-robot collaboration, and change the way we approach problem-solving. It's an exciting and transformative time as we continue to explore the possibilities of large language models and their integration into our lives.
