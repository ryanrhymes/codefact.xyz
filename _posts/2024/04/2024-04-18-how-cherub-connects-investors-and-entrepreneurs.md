As the co-founder of [Pathfinder Ventures](https://www.pathfinder.ventures/), I have long pondered the best ways to efficiently discover the right startups that align with my investment focus. Enter Cherub, a new platform that appears to redefine how investors and entrepreneurs interconnect – and I was eager to uncover the specifics behind their innovative matching process.

![Image](https://assets-global.website-files.com/63f65e36f83418180b98ca68/65a09a905e8303fd692b3c4d_cherub_coming_soon.png "getty")

The question burning at the forefront of my mind was, "How does Cherub's matching process work between investors and entrepreneurs, and what sets it apart as a valuable proposition for both parties?" I regarded this inquiry as the most crucial one to answer, as gaining insight into the answer could potentially lead me to significant time and resource savings while also boosting my potential for savvy investment decisions.

At its essence, Cherub operates as a marketplace, empowering entrepreneurs to connect with suitably aligned angel investors. Similar to how dating platforms pair individuals based on compatibility, Cherub employs similar mechanisms, matching entrepreneurs with investors based on shared industry focus, funding stages, and investment preferences.

For entrepreneurs, the benefits lie in the efficiency of finding investors who fit their unique needs. By filtering their investment interests, entrepreneurs are presented with matches tailored specifically to their industries and funding stages. This targeted approach significantly increases their chances of securing crucial funding while also forming more fruitful partnerships between investor and entrepreneur.

From the investors' point of view, the benefits are equally advantageous. Being presented with high-quality, relevant deal flow that aligns with their investment focus saves them precious time and resources, allowing them to concentrate their efforts on evaluating promising opportunities. The gatekeeper role that Cherub assumes, filtering out irrelevant opportunities and presenting the most suitable matches, further minimizes the risk of making suboptimal investment decisions.

In essence, imagine a world where investors could simply log onto a platform and be introduced to a carefully curated list of startups that perfectly align with their investment interests and criteria. This is the value proposition that Cherub offers, pioneering a streamlined, optimized, and efficient investment process that benefits both entrepreneurs and investors alike.

In conclusion, Cherub's innovative matching process holds pivotal importance in uniting investors and entrepreneurs, delivering a game-changing solution to the industry. By understanding the inner workings and benefits of this process, angel investors and entrepreneurs have the opportunity to significantly enhance their respective businesses and collaborations.
