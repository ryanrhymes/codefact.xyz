Manufacturing and agriculture industries are confronted with an escalating challenge in today's market: labor shortages. In response, AI-driven automation is quickly becoming the go-to solution, and one Belgian startup, Robovision, is making a significant impact in this arena with its innovative computer vision solutions tailored for both industries.

![Image](https://robovision.ai/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/image-website-robovision-ilvo-AI-drone-case-study-web.webp "getty")

Accessibility is the cornerstone of Robovision's success. Its platform, built upon a "no-code" approach, eliminates the necessity of employing large in-house tech teams for businesses seeking to adopt its solutions. Many manufacturing and agriculture firms, though, do not possess a tech focus, making the traditionally challenging adoption of new technologies a steep uphill battle. By addressing this issue, Robovision empowers businesses to streamline their processes, boost productivity, and remain competitive in a thriving automation market.

Robovision's computer vision AI platform offers a range of applications within manufacturing and agriculture. Data collection, crop monitoring, and harvesting processes are just a few examples of how the technology can transform these industries. For agriculture, Robovision's AI-powered platform can pinpoint crop diseases in real-time, enabling farmers to act promptly to prevent the spread of issues and cultivate healthier crops – ultimately resulting in impressive yield gains. In manufacturing, real-time defect identification through computer vision technology reduces the release of faulty goods, minimizing waste and saving resources.

Beyond merely filling labor gaps, Robovision's computer vision solutions are set to revolutionize industries and redefine how we produce and process goods. In industrial manufacturing, the adoption of cutting-edge computer vision technologies is ushering in the era of Industry 4.0, or Smart Factories, offering real-time insights and automating intricate processes. In agriculture, precision farming and animal welfare monitoring, powered by computer vision solutions, optimize resource usage and improve animal wellbeing.

Robovision's accomplishments extend beyond the European market, with recent substantial funding enabling its entry into the US market. Its customer base currently includes over 45 countries, and the company confidently embraces the opportunities presented within this vast market.

Robovision's computer vision AI platform is a pivotal player reshaping the manufacturing and agriculture industries. By prioritizing accessibility, Robovision dismantles long-standing barriers to AI adoption within non-tech-centric businesses. Its solutions address labor shortages, streamline operations, and drive unparalleled productivity gains, all while paving the way for a more data-driven, interconnected future. As automation and artificial intelligence increasingly come to the forefront of our global economy, companies like Robovision will be at the vanguard, leading the charge towards a more efficient and optimized world.
